Refund Policy

Last updated September 25, 2026

The short version: the first two lessons are free, so you can try the course before you pay. Because you get instant access to all the lessons, we don't give refunds for changing your mind. If something goes wrong on our side, like a double charge or not being able to get into the course, we'll make it right.

Try before you buy

Lessons 1 and 2 are completely free with an account, along with their quizzes. Please try them before buying, so you know the teaching style works for you.

When you can get a refund

We'll give you a full refund if:

  • You were charged twice for the same course, or bought it twice by accident.
  • You paid but can't access the course, and we can't fix it within 3 business days of you contacting us.
  • The payment wasn't made by you or someone allowed to use your card. Contact us within 30 days.
  • The course is shut down within 12 months of your purchase without the 60 days' notice promised in our Terms.

When refunds aren't available

Because the whole course unlocks right after you pay, we don't offer refunds for changing your mind, not having time, or having finished the course. This applies except where the law requires otherwise.

How to ask

  1. Message us from the help button inside the course, or email esdrasbuelvas31@gmail.com.
  2. Use the email address you bought with, and tell us what happened.
  3. We'll reply within 2 business days. Approved refunds go back to your original payment method through Square, usually within 5–10 business days. Access to the paid lessons ends when the refund is processed.

Please talk to us first

If something's wrong, message us before disputing the charge with your bank. We can almost always fix it faster, and disputes can take weeks to resolve.
